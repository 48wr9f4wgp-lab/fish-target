# FISH TARGET — FISH VISUAL ART BIBLE V34

Status: Canonical visual direction for target-fish assets
Applies to: Home target cards, result hero/detail, offline bundled assets

## 1. Visual job

Fish imagery is not decoration. It must do three jobs:
1. help the user identify a target quickly;
2. make the product feel coherent and trustworthy;
3. remain safe to bundle and display offline.

Species identity has priority over drama, photorealism or visual novelty.

## 2. Canonical direction

- Natural-history / premium field-guide realism.
- Clean isolated subject; no scenery, angler, hook, blood, ruler, text or watermark in the art.
- Predominantly lateral profile with only slight perspective when needed to show diagnostic anatomy.
- Consistent visual weight across cards; do not scale a small fish into a visually larger animal than a large species merely to fill the frame.
- Neutral transparent or near-transparent presentation suitable for existing card backgrounds.
- Natural, restrained saturation. Preserve diagnostic bars, spots, lateral lines, fin colors and body proportions.
- Adult/typical angling appearance unless the product target explicitly refers to a life stage.
- No fantasy anatomy, exaggerated teeth, trophy distortion or cinematic lighting.

## 3. Composition contract

For ordinary fish:
- horizontal composition
- head consistently faces right
- full body including tail and fins visible
- 6–10% safe margin around anatomy
- eye and mouth remain legible at card size
- no fin or tail clipping in 375px layouts

For flatfish:
- recognizable eyed-side silhouette
- orientation must preserve head/eye placement rather than forcing ordinary-fish pose

For squid/cuttlefish/octopus:
- preserve mantle, fins/arms and characteristic silhouette
- rotate only enough to fit the same horizontal card grammar

For crustaceans/other non-fish targets:
- use the same neutral field-guide treatment rather than imitating fish anatomy/composition.

## 4. Compound/generic product labels

Never bind a single falsely-specific image to an ambiguous label merely because it looks plausible.

Examples:
- `ブリ・ワラサ`: same species / growth-stage product family; one representative species illustration is acceptable if the label remains explicit.
- `ヤマメ・イワナ`: two distinct target taxa; use a deliberate dual-subject treatment or split the product target later. Do not show only one and imply both.
- broad labels such as `カレイ`: require an intentional generic/family treatment or taxonomy/product resolution before exact-species promotion.
- unresolved taxonomy records stay on a safe fallback until resolved.

## 5. Asset tiers

Tier A — publication bundled
- provenance/ownership clear
- offline safe
- canonical crop and QA passed

Tier B — verified candidate / intake pending
- source, taxon, license and attribution reviewed
- not yet publication bundled until receipt/hash/transformation gates pass

Tier C — safe product fallback
- original project illustration/SVG or deliberately generic visual
- must not assert a false species identity

Tier D — remote licensed fallback
- optional enhancement only
- core target selection must not depend on network access

## 6. Current V34 gap

The V34 baseline has 63 targets but the legacy bundled authoring contains only 19 sprite targets. Those legacy records also have unknown license and unverified rights metadata, so they are a visual-development asset, not sufficient publication provenance.

V34 must therefore treat visual completion as both an art-consistency problem and a rights/provenance problem.

## 7. Review rubric per target

A target is visually complete only when all are true:
- identity: recognizably the intended target at small size
- anatomy: no obvious species-shape error
- diagnostic marks: important color/marking cues preserved
- crop: full meaningful silhouette remains visible
- consistency: matches the shared field-guide direction
- accessibility: readable against light and dark-ish card regions
- offline: bundled or safe fallback exists
- taxonomy: exactness matches the product label
- rights: publication status is explicit and auditable

## 8. Production sequence

1. resolve product/taxonomy ambiguity
2. create/review visual reference for one representative saltwater fish, one freshwater fish, one flatfish and one cephalopod
3. approve shared style against actual 390px cards
4. produce the high-value launch cluster
5. run identity/crop review
6. expand in batches, never 63 unreviewed images at once
7. run 375/390/430 and physical iPhone review

## 9. Prohibited shortcuts

- no image scraping without provenance
- no watermarked/unknown-rights publication asset
- no one-image-for-two-species shortcut except an explicit dual composition
- no replacing scientific identity with a generic 'cool fish'
- no visual style mix of photo, cartoon and unrelated render styles on the primary target grid
