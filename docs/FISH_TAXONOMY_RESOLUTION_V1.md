## Rights boundary
This resolution registry is research/QA metadata only. It cannot carry `license`, `rights_status`, or `publication_ready`, and it cannot change an image candidate to `verified-candidate`.

All six existing image-candidate rows remain `taxonomy-review` until a matching candidate with supported license and attribution is independently verified. Third-party binary intake remains a separate explicit-approval gate.
